What it does

01

Import students

From Compass, Sentral, SIMON and others, with surname capitals fixed and the tricky ones flagged.

02

Allocate in one go

By year level, group and surname, with a draft you check before anything changes.

03

Codes done properly

Codes follow lock makers’ advice, are not given back to a lock that had them in the last three years, stay hidden until asked for, and every reveal is recorded.

04

Labels that line up

Exact millimetres on Avery sheets, your own measurements, and a calibration page for each printer.

05

Letters, one per page

Each student’s locker (and code, when their lock has one), with the right steps for their lock, in more than one language.

06

The whole year

New students (imported or added by hand), leavers, moves and swaps, locks to reset, lost codes, reports, Undo after every change, and a guided start to next year.

Big school or small

A school that only needs to record who has which locker can do just that. Quick start makes an area, a bank and its lockers in one step. Keyed locks, or no locks, mean no codes to manage. Students can be added by hand, without an import, and labels and letters can be set aside with “We don’t use this”.

Your data stays at school

  • One data file in a folder your school chooses, with backups beside it.
  • No server, no accounts, no data sent anywhere. Updates come from GitHub.
  • Lock codes are stored encrypted in the file and hidden on screen, and the school can add a PIN.
  • Open source under the MIT licence: anyone can check what it does.

Getting started

  1. Download the installer for Windows or Mac from the download page.
  2. The first time, follow the short security steps on the download page. You only do this once.
  3. Try the demo school, then start your school’s file.
