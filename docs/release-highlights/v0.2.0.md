You can now set up your school: its name, colours and logo (with a black-and-white version made for labels), the words your school uses (Homeroom, Form, Roll class and so on), and all your lockers, banks and areas. The new Lockers screen shows each bank as it stands in the corridor. Lockers can be marked out of service, reserved, accessible or shared, and renumbered only through a deliberate tool.
