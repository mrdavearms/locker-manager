Locker Manager can now give out lockers and lock codes. Allocate a whole year in one go by your rules (for example Year 7 to the Year 7 side, by Homeroom then surname, HUB last), check the draft, swap anyone by clicking, then commit. Codes follow the lock makers' advice, are stored scrambled, and are shown only on request, which is recorded. New students, leavers, moves, swaps and new codes each take a few clicks, and locks that need a physical reset are listed until you tick them off. Press Ctrl+K to find anyone, and Ctrl+Z to undo.
