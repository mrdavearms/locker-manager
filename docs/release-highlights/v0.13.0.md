Locker Manager now asks each school to register once. Registration is free. It lets Dave Armstrong, who makes Locker Manager, know which schools use it.

**Register before you update, if you can.** Dave checks each registration by hand and usually replies within a day. After you update, a copy with no key opens your school's file look-only until the key arrives. You can still look up students and lockers, print lists and export without codes. You cannot change anything, show or print codes, or make a new file.

**How to register:** open Locker Manager and click **Register this school**, or use the form at https://mrdavearms.github.io/locker-manager/register/. Type your name, school, email address and state. You get a key by email. Paste it in once and click **Register**.

- **One key covers your whole school.** It is saved in your school's file, so every computer that opens the file is registered.
- **IT can put the key on every computer** with a `registration.key` file. The IT guide has the folder.
- **The demo school and updates always work**, with or without a key.
- **Your privacy:** registering sends your name, school, email address, state and optional role, once, when you click Send. Nothing from your school's file is ever sent, and the app never sends anything to check a key.
