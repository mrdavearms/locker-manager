You can now import students from your student system: Compass, Sentral and others, as CSV, Excel or pasted from a spreadsheet. The app recognises the export, fixes surnames that arrive in capitals (McNair, O'Brien), and asks you to check the ones it cannot be sure of (MacDonald, De La Rue). Before anything changes it shows new students, changes and possible leavers, and it never removes anyone by itself. A student who appears in the wrong year's file is held back for you to decide.
